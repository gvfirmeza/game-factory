# Game Brief: Grove Odyssey

## High Concept
A complete, cozy, non-violent exploratory mini Metroidvania. You play as **Lumi**, a tiny glowing forest spirit exploring the enchanted realm of Grove Odyssey. The ancient Great Elder Tree has lost its 8 Ancient Sun Seeds across 7 interconnected subterranean and canopy zones.

## Core Pillars
1. **Rich Interconnected World (7 Zones)**:
   - Heart Grove (Central Hub)
   - Mossy Caverns (West Deep)
   - Shimmering Crystal Grotto (East Low)
   - Sunlit Canopy (North High)
   - Forgotten Sunken Roots (South Deep)
   - Windy Chasm (North East Highlands)
   - Secret Elder Shrine (Hidden Vault)
2. **True Metroidvania Progression (3 Abilities & Gates)**:
   - *Feather Jump* (Double Jump) -> unlocks high cliffs in Canopy.
   - *Wind Glide* (Slow Fall / Hover) -> unlocks Windy Chasm crossings.
   - *Leaf Dash* (Quick horizontal burst) -> unlocks Thorn Barriers and fragile crystal walls.
3. **Interactive Woodland NPCs with Zero-Overflow Dialogue**:
   - Barnaby the Snail (Guide), Bramble the Hedgehog (Miner/Hermit), Pip the Owl (Sage).
4. **Content Density**:
   - 8 Collectible Ancient Sun Seeds.
   - 3 Enemy / Hazard types (Bramble Slimes, Shadow Wisps, Thorn Beetles) with damage knockback & invulnerability frames.
   - 3 Ancient Waystone Checkpoints.
