# Game Brief: Tidebound

## High-Level Concept
- **Title**: Tidebound
- **Genre**: 2D Platform Adventure / Cozy Island Exploration
- **Target Platform**: Playgama (Mobile & Desktop HTML5)
- **Target Session**: 20–35 min playthrough, 30–45 min 100% exploration
- **Tone**: Cozy, adventurous, colorful, responsive, playful, mysterious, polished.

## Protagonist
- **Name**: Cori the Reef Sprite (or Nauti)
- **Visuals**: Turquoise/cyan coastal sprite with seafoam cape, nautilus shell beret, luminescent pearl antennae, expressive blink/emote eyes.
- **Mobility**: Ground run (200px/s), variable jump (-390px/s), coyote time (0.10s), jump buffer (0.12s), 1x Tide Dash (450px/s for 0.18s, unlocked in Area 3), Stomp attack rebound (-320px/s).

## 5 Distinct World Levels
1. **Area 1 — Palm Beach**: Gentle sandbanks, palm boughs, Hermit Scuttler enemy, Sun Pearls, Checkpoint, 1st Lighthouse.
2. **Area 2 — Overgrown Ruins**: Ancient stone arches, moving mossy platforms, Barnaby the Navigator NPC, 2nd Lighthouse.
3. **Area 3 — Flooded Caves**: Geysers, disappearing coral ledges, Spiny Urchins, Bubble Ray Flyer enemy, Shrine of the Tide Dash (ability unlock).
4. **Area 4 — Wind Cliffs**: Coastal updrafts, moving cloud ledges, Charging Coral Crab enemy, 3rd Lighthouse.
5. **Area 5 — Lighthouse Island & Boss Arena**: Climax obstacle course, The Ancient Tide Golem boss (3 phases), Final Lighthouse activation, Ending Altar.

## Key Progression & Collectibles
- **Lighthouses**: 3 Ancient Lighthouses + 1 Final Beacon.
- **Sun Pearls**: 25 Collectibles (5 per area) giving +50 pts each.
- **Golden Nautilus Shells**: 50 Pickups (10 per area) giving +20 pts each.
- **Tide Medallions**: 5 Hidden Lore Medallions (+500 pts each).
- **NPCs**: 3 Interactive NPCs (Barnaby the Navigator, Coralia the Pearl Diver, Ancient Beacon Keeper).
