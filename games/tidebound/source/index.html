📖 How to Play

🏃 Movement & Jump

Use A/D or Arrow Keys to run. Press Space or W to jump. Release early for variable jump heights!

🌊 Tide Dash

Press Shift, J, or X in mid-air for a hydro-powered dash! You get 1 Air Dash per jump, restored by landing or stomping enemies.

💥 Enemy Stomp

Land directly on top of enemies to bounce skyward (-320px/s), defeating pests and instantly resetting your Tide Dash!

☀️ Sun Pearls & Lighthouses

Collect all 25 Sun Pearls, discover 5 Hidden Lore Medallions, and ignite 4 Lighthouses to calm the Ancient Tide Golem!
