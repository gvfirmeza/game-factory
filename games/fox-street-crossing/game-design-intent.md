# Game Design Intent: Fox Street Crossing

## 1. Core Experience & Emotional Target
Player feels a rush of adrenaline from quick reflex gameplay, with satisfaction from successfully navigating tight gaps between vehicles. The cute fox aesthetic provides charm, while the increasing traffic density creates escalating tension. Core enjoyment comes from the "one more try" loop of brief, intense runs.

## 2. Core Gameplay Loop
[Dodge traffic] -> [Near-miss adrenaline] -> [Collect apple] -> [Points/reward] -> [Progress to next level with faster traffic]

## 3. Primary Player Verb
[Dash/step] between moving vehicles in a single lane, timing movements to avoid collisions while targeting apple collectibles.

## 4. Mechanic Purpose Contract

### Mechanic: Lane Switching
- **PURPOSE**: Allow player to move between lanes to find safe gaps in traffic flow.
- **TEACHING**: First level introduces two lanes with slow-moving cars; player learns to time presses between vehicles.
- **APPLICATION**: Player switches lanes across 3-4 lane roads with increasing traffic density.
- **ESCALATION**: Traffic speeds up; vehicles include faster cars and slower trucks requiring different timing.
- **MASTERY**: Perfect runs through maximum-density traffic without single collision; chaining multiple apple collections in a single run.

### Mechanic: Apple Collection
- **PURPOSE**: Provide primary scoring and 1-up thresholds; reward risk-taking to enter tighter gaps.
- **TEACHING**: First apple appears in widest gap; collectible teaches player to balance risk/reward.
- **APPLICATION**: Apples appear throughout subsequent levels; some in challenging positions requiring careful lane switching.
- **ESCALATION**: Apples appear in more dangerous positions as traffic speeds up; higher point values at speed.
- **MASTERY**: Collecting maximum apples in a single run while maintaining collision-free progression.

### Mechanic: Vehicle Dodge Patterns
- **PURPOSE**: Create predictable yet challenging obstacles that require timing and pattern recognition.
- **TEACHING**: First car moves slowly in a straight line; player learns to wait for passage.
- **APPLICATION**: Cars move in varying patterns (straight, weaving); player must observe and time movements.
- **ESCALATION**: Mix of car speeds, truck sizes, and occasional bus obstacles; some vehicles change lanes.
- **MASTERY**: Reading and predicting complex vehicle patterns under time pressure.

## 5. Player Learning Progression
- **First 10 Seconds**: Understand controls (lane switching), recognize car types, collect first apple.
- **First 30 Seconds**: Time movements between moving vehicles, avoid first collision, understand score.
- **First 2 Minutes**: Navigate 3+ lane roads, recognize vehicle patterns, collect apples efficiently.
- **End Game**: Master highest traffic density levels, optimize routes for apple collection, achieve perfect runs.

## 6. Zero Filler Content Rule
- No NPCs, dialogue, shops, or secondary mechanics included - all elements directly serve the core crossing gameplay.
- Vehicles and apples are the only collectibles/hazards; each serves the core loop of dodging and scoring.
