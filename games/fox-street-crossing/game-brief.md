# Game Brief: Fox Street Crossing

## High Concept
A cute arcade game where a small fox crosses a busy street collecting apples while avoiding cars and trucks. Quick reflexes and timing are essential as traffic speeds up across multiple levels.

## Core Gameplay
1. **Street Crossing**: Guide the fox from the bottom of the screen to the top, crossing multiple lanes of traffic
2. **Collectibles**: Apples scattered across the road provide points and extra lives at thresholds
3. **Hazards**: Cars, trucks, and other vehicles move at varying speeds; contact resets the fox to start
4. **Progression**: Each level increases traffic density and speed, adding new vehicle types

## Levels
- Level 1: Two-lane road, slow traffic, introduction of car types
- Level 2: Three-lane road, mixed car and truck traffic
- Level 3: Four-lane highway, fast traffic, introduced moving obstacles
- Level 5: Rush hour - densest traffic, fastest speeds

## Core Loop
1. Start at bottom lane
2. Dodge traffic and navigate gaps
3. Collect apples for points
4. Reach top safe zone to advance to next level
5. Avoid collisions, manage lives

## Visual Style
- Cute, cartoonish aesthetic
- Fox protagonist with expressive animations
- Vehicles with varying designs (cars, trucks, buses)
- Apple collectibles with visual glow
- Street environment with lane markings and roadside elements

## Audio
- Light, upbeat background music
- Sound effects for apple collection, vehicle passing, and collision
- Feedback cues for near-misses
