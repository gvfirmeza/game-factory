# Game Brief: Orbit Guard

## 1. High Concept
**Orbit Guard** is a mobile-first, high-octane circular arena merge tower defense and auto-battler. Set on a celestial defense platform surrounding a dying star's planetary **Nexus Core**, players summon, place, and merge elemental defense sentinels along concentric orbital rings to repel spiraling waves of cosmic void invaders and multi-phase titan bosses.

---

## 2. Setting & Theme
- **Setting**: Deep-space planetary defense orbital platform (*The Celestial Aegis*).
- **Aesthetic**: Neon sci-fi with vibrant glowing energy rings, crisp particle lasers, pulsing plasma explosions, and dark void entity silhouettes.
- **Tone**: Intense, tactical, arcade satisfaction with instant tactile feedback.

---

## 3. Core Gameplay Pillars

### 1. Concentric Circular Arena Defense
Unlike traditional linear lane tower defense, combat unfolds in a 360-degree radial space. Enemies spawn on outer orbital tracks and spiral inward toward the central Nexus Core. Defense sentinels placed on the inner orbital ring possess distinct angular ranges, trajectory arcs, and targeting behaviors to defend the core from all approach vectors.

### 2. Snappy Drag-and-Drop Merge Economy
Players summon Level 1 sentinels using gold earned from fallen invaders. Merging two identical same-tier sentinels (Lv 1 + Lv 1 $\rightarrow$ Lv 2, up to Lv 6+ Ascended) instantly synthesizes a vastly more powerful unit with enhanced fire rates, larger splash radiuses, chain-lightning counts, or lethal critical strike perks.

### 3. Asymmetric Tactical Roles (5 Archetypes)
Five distinct troop archetypes create rich situational counter-play:
1. **Ballista Archer (Railgun Sentry)**: Single-target, long-range sniper with rapid kinetic piercing.
2. **Heavy Cannon (Plasma Mortar)**: Long-range, slow cadence, massive area-of-effect splash damage against dense clusters.
3. **Arcane Mage (Tesla Caster)**: Mid-range multi-target chain lightning that arcs between closely grouped enemies.
4. **Frost Warden (Cryo Emitter)**: $360^\circ$ cryogenic slowing aura that chills and halts fast rushers.
5. **Shadow Assassin (Void Ripper)**: Short-range perimeter blade specialist with devastating $3\times$ critical strike burst damage.

### 4. Relentless Void Enemy Incursions & Titan Bosses
Five distinct enemy types test defensive composition:
- **Void Crawler**: Standard paced swarm footman.
- **Swift Dart**: High-velocity sprinter that rushes past slow-firing artillery.
- **Armored Bruiser**: Heavy high-HP tank that shrugs off light physical strikes.
- **Swarm Pod**: Carrier organism that splits into 4-6 micro-mites on death.
- **Void Slinger**: Ranged artillery unit that bombards defenses from the outer perimeter.
Every 5 waves features a colossal Titan Boss with unique mechanics (**Iron Colossus**, **Hydra Queen**, **Chrono Wraith**).

### 5. Mobile-First Ergonomics & Arcade Purity
Designed ground-up for one-handed portrait touch controls (and mouse/trackpad drag on desktop). Zero mandatory filler dialogue or non-diegetic bloat—every second is focused on tactical merge decisions, wave surges, and escalating orbital defense.
