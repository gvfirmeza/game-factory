HOW TO PLAY

Game Rules & Combat Guide

DRAG TO MERGE

Drag one unit on top of another identical unit to level it up (Tier 1 to Tier 6) and multiply its attack damage.

FOCUS TARGET LOCK

Tap any enemy or boss on screen to immediately focus-fire all your turrets onto that target.

DEFEND THE CORE

Enemies spiral inward toward the center base. Don't let them reach your core HP! Use Ion Strike if overwhelmed.

RESONANCE BEAMS

Place complementary sentinels in adjacent ring slots to ignite laser barriers that zap passing invaders.

BENCH AND RECYCLE

Store extra units on the bottom standby bench. Drag unwanted units to the Recycler on the right for 70% refund.
